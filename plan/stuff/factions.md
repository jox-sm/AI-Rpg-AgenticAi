# RPG Factions

## Good Factions

### The Radiant Order
- **ID**: radiant_order
- **Alignment**: Good
- **Leader**: High Paladin Aldric Stormshield
- **Headquarters**: The Golden Citadel, Holy City of Solara
- **Members**: Holy knights, paladins, and clerics sworn to protect the innocent
- **Goals**: Defend the realm from evil, spread light and justice, vanquish darkness
- **Allies**: Church of Light, Silver Dawn Rangers
- **Enemies**: Shadow Covenant, Crimson Skull Legion, Void Seekers
- **Reputation Rewards**:
  - Rank 1 (Acolyte): Minor healing potions, blessed trinkets
  - Rank 2 (Knight): Radiant Armor set, holy mount
  - Rank 3 (Champion): Legendary holy weapon, divine aura ability
  - Rank 4 (High Lord): Command of Paladin squad, access to Sacred Vault
