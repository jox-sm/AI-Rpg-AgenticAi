- **Destruction**: Offensive spells dealing direct damage

- **Enchantment**: Item and creature enhancement magic

## Spell ID Naming Convention

All spell IDs follow the format `snake_case` using lowercase letters and underscores. Examples:
- `fire_bolt`
- `greater_heal`
- `raise_skeleton`
- `arcane_intellect`

## Stat Requirements

- **INT** (Intelligence): Required for most offensive and utility spells
- **WIS** (Wisdom): Required for healing and divine magic
- **CON** (Constitution): Required for some defensive spells
- **STR** (Strength): Required for physical enhancement spells
- **DEX** (Dexterity): Required for stealth and speed spells
- **CHA** (Charisma): Required for charm and persuasion spells
