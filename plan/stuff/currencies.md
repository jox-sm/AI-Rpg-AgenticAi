# RPG Currencies

---

## Coins

### Copper Coin
- **ID**: copper_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 0.1 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 copper = 1/10 silver, 1/100 gold
- **Source**: Quest rewards, looting bodies, selling junk items
- **Use**: Buying basic supplies, bread, ale, mundane tools
- **Description**: A small, dull reddish-brown coin stamped with an anvil. Worn smooth from years of use.

### Silver Coin
- **ID**: silver_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 1 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 silver = 10 copper, 1/10 gold
- **Source**: Mid-tier quest rewards, selling crafted goods, dungeon loot
- **Use**: Armor repair, weapon sharpening, inn stays, moderate supplies
- **Description**: A bright coin with a reflective sheen, bearing the profile of a long-dead queen. Edges are slightly notched.

### Gold Coin
- **ID**: gold_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 10 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 gold = 10 silver, 100 copper
- **Source**: High-tier quests, rare monster drops, treasure chests
- **Use**: Equipment purchases, property transactions, hiring mercenaries
- **Description**: A heavy, lustrous coin stamped with a dragon clutching a gem. The standard currency of the realm.

### Electrum Coin
- **ID**: electrum_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 5 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 electrum = 5 gold, 50 silver
- **Source**: Ancient ruins, buried treasure, rare merchant trades
- **Use**: Luxury goods, enchanted items, rare ingredients
- **Description**: A pale gold-silver alloy coin with a faint magical shimmer. Tingles when held. Stamped with an eye.

### Platinum Coin
- **ID**: platinum_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 100 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 platinum = 10 gold, 1000 silver
- **Source**: Legendary quests, dragon hoards, royal treasury
- **Use**: Legendary equipment, castle purchases, kingdom-level transactions
- **Description**: A dense, silvery-white coin that resists tarnish. Stamped with a crowned phoenix. Only the wealthy handle these.

### Mithril Coin
- **ID**: mithril_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 500 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 mithril = 5 platinum, 50 gold
- **Source**: Elven trade, ancient dragon lairs, celestial quests
- **Use**: Artisan masterworks, magical reagents, high enchantments
- **Description**: An impossibly light coin of blue-tinged silver that hums faintly. Stamped with an elven tree. Practically weightless.

### Dark Coin
- **ID**: dark_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 25 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 dark = 2.5 gold, 25 silver
- **Source**: Shadow markets, undead lairs, necromancer dealings
- **Use**: Black market purchases, forbidden scrolls, undead services
- **Description**: A coin that seems to absorb light. Stamped with a skull wreathed in smoke. Cold to the touch even in fire.

### Dragon Coin
- **ID**: dragon_coin
- **Type**: Coin
- **Value**: 200 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 dragon = 2 platinum, 20 gold
- **Source**: Dragon kin, volcanic forges, ancient treasures
- **Use**: Dragon-related enchantments, fire resistance gear, elite services
- **Description**: A coin of dragon scale alloy, warm to the touch, stamped with a roaring dragon head. Smells faintly of sulfur.

## Tokens

### Arena Token
- **ID**: arena_token
- **Type**: Token
- **Value**: 5 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 arena = 5 gold
- **Source**: Winning arena fights, tournament participation
- **Use**: Arena betting, exclusive combat gear, challenge rights
- **Description**: A thick bronze medallion stamped with crossed swords. Dented from the arena. Smells of sweat and sawdust.

### Guild Token
- **ID**: guild_token
- **Type**: Token
- **Value**: 10 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 guild = 10 gold
- **Source**: Completing guild contracts, guild rank advancement
- **Use**: Guild shop purchases, training access, guild hall services
- **Description**: A polished wooden token with the guild seal inlaid in brass. Each guild has its own unique design.

### Merchant Token
- **ID**: merchant_token
- **Type**: Token
- **Value**: 15 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 merchant = 15 gold
- **Source**: Trade routes, merchant quests, caravan escorts
- **Use**: Bulk purchasing discounts, trade route access, market stalls
- **Description**: A square copper token stamped with scales and a ship. Issued by the Merchant Council for verified traders.

### Bounty Token
- **ID**: bounty_token

- **Value**: 20 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 bounty = 20 gold
- **Source**: Completing bounty contracts, monster hunting
- **Use**: Bounty board access, monster hunter gear, tracking tools
- **Description**: A blood-red token stamped with a skull and crosshair. Carries the seal of the Bounty Hunter's Guild.

### Royal Token
- **ID**: royal_token
- **Type**: Token
- **Value**: 50 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 royal = 5 gold
- **Source**: Royal favor, exceptional service to the crown
- **Use**: Royal court access, noble petitions, royal merchant discounts
- **Description**: A gold-plated token stamped with the royal crest and signed by the monarch. Rare and highly respected.

### Pit Token

- **Value**: 8 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 pit = 8 gold

## Magical

### Mana Crystal
- **ID**: mana_crystal
- **Type**: Magical
- **Value**: 30 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 mana = 3 gold, 30 silver
- **Source**: Mana springs, arcane creatures, enchanting
- **Use**: Spellcasting fuel, magical crafting, mana restoration
- **Description**: A translucent blue crystal that pulses with inner light. Glows brighter near ley lines. Warm to the touch.

### Soul Essence
- **ID**: soul_essence
- **Type**: Magical
- **Value**: 75 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 soul = 7.5 gold, 75 silver
- **Source**: Defeated spirits, necromantic rituals, soulbinding
- **Use**: Necromancy, soulbinding contracts, dark enchantments
- **Description**: A swirling orb of ghostly energy contained in a glass vial. Whispers faintly. Cold and unsettling to hold.

### Arcane Dust
- **ID**: arcane_dust

- **Value**: 12 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 arcane = 1.2 gold, 12 silver
- **Source**: Shattered magical items, arcane slimes, magic workshops
- **Use**: Enchanting, potion brewing, scroll crafting
- **Description**: Fine glittering powder that shifts color in light. Floats upward if left uncovered. Tingles against skin.

### Celestial Shard

- **Source**: Celestial beings, meteor strikes, divine quests
- **Use**: Holy enchantments, divine spellcasting, celestial forging

### Void Fragment

- **Source**: Rift zones, void creatures, planar travel

### Dragon Tear
- **ID**: dragon_tear
- **Type**: Magical
- **Value**: 250 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 tear = 25 gold, 250 silver
- **Source**: Elder dragons, dragon nests, ancient dragon relics
- **Use**: Fire/frost enchantments, dragon armor crafting, powerful potions

## Special

### Reputation Badge
- **ID**: reputation_badge

- **Value**: 0 gold (non-tradable)
- **Exchange Rate**: Cannot be exchanged
- **Source**: Completing major story quests, heroic deeds
- **Use**: Unlocking faction discounts, special dialogues, unique quests
- **Description**: A brass pin stamped with a star. Each faction has its own design. Proof of your deeds and trustworthiness.

### Honor Token
- **ID**: honor_token
- **Type**: Special
- **Value**: 0 gold (non-tradable)
- **Exchange Rate**: Cannot be exchanged
- **Source**: Winning honorable duels, protecting NPCs, chivalric deeds
- **Use**: Entering restricted areas, gaining NPC trust, paladin training
- **Description**: A gleaming white token stamped with a sword and shield. Resonates with righteous energy. Glows in the presence of evil.

### Corruption Shard
- **ID**: corruption_shard
- **Type**: Special
- **Value**: 0 gold (non-tradable)
- **Exchange Rate**: Cannot be exchanged
- **Source**: Embracing dark choices, serving evil factions, dark rituals
- **Use**: Accessing evil vendors, dark magic training, forbidden quests
- **Description**: A pulsing red-black crystal that whispers temptations. Grows larger with each dark deed. Cannot be discarded.

### Fate Coin
- **ID**: fate_coin
- **Type**: Special
- **Value**: 0 gold (non-tradable)
- **Exchange Rate**: Cannot be exchanged
- **Source**: Fate quests, destiny events, rare random encounters
- **Use**: Altering destiny, rerolling critical events, changing outcomes
- **Description**: A two-headed coin — one side shows a sun, the other a moon. Spins on its own. Seems to know things.

### Veteran Mark
- **ID**: veteran_mark
- **Type**: Special
- **Value**: 0 gold (non-tradable)
- **Exchange Rate**: Cannot be exchanged
- **Source**: Completing all quests in a region, maxing faction reputation
- **Use**: Unique vendor access, exclusive cosmetics, legendary training
- **Description**: A scar-shaped brand that appears on your hand. Cannot be removed. Recognized by all as a mark of true experience.

### Legendary Token
- **ID**: legendary_token
- **Type**: Special
- **Value**: 1000 gold equivalent
- **Exchange Rate**: 1 legendary = 10 platinum, 100 gold
- **Source**: Legendary quests, world bosses, secret endings
- **Use**: Legendary equipment, unique abilities, world-altering choices
- **Description**: A radiant gold token with the seal of the gods. Only 99 exist in the world. Empowers the bearer with divine favor.
