## Common Diseases

### Common Cold
- **ID**: common_cold
- **Type**: Viral
- **Symptoms**: Sneezing, runny nose, mild congestion, sore throat
- **Duration**: 3-5 days
- **Cure**: Rest, restorative potion, or natural recovery
- **Transmission**: Airborne droplets, contact with infected surfaces
- **Severity**: Mild
- **Effects**: -1 Agility, -2 Charisma
