# Minerals & Ores Database

Complete mineral and ore reference for the text-based RPG system. All minerals follow the standardized format for consistency in crafting, trading, and world-building mechanics.

## Category 1: Metals (55 Minerals)

### Copper
- **ID**: copper
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Hardness**: 3
- **Value**: 5 gold
- **Spawn Level**: 1
- **Biome**: Plains, Forests, Hills
- **Used For**: Basic weapons, armor, tools, wiring
- **Special Properties**: Excellent conductor of electricity and heat. Tarnishes over time forming green patina.

- **Hardness**: 2
