## Metals

### Copper Ore
- **ID**: copper_ore
- **Category**: Metal
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Source**: Mined from surface deposits, shallow caves
- **Value**: 2 gold
- **Used For**: Copper tools, copper weapons, bronze alloy
- **Stackable**: Yes
- **Description**: A rough chunk of reddish-brown ore with a dull metallic luster. The most basic metal used by beginning smiths.

### Tin Ore
