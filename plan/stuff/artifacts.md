# RPG Artifacts Collection

## Weapon Artifacts

### Sword of Destiny
- **ID**: sword_of_destiny
- **Type**: Weapon
- **Rarity**: Legendary
- **Power**: Deals triple damage to bosses and grants +50% critical hit chance
- **Curse**: Binds to wielder upon first use; cannot be dropped or traded
- **Origin**: Forged by the gods during the First Age to slay the Eternal Dragon
- **Quest Required**: The Prophecy of Shadows
- **Level Required**: 25
- **Description**: A gleaming longsword with a blade of pure starlight, its hilt wrapped in phoenix feather leather. Runes of destiny glow faintly along the edge.

### Shadowfang Dagger
- **ID**: shadowfang_dagger
- **Type**: Weapon
- **Rarity**: Mythic
- **Power**: Attacks ignore armor and deal poison damage over 10 seconds
- **Curse**: Drains 5% of wielder's max health with each strike
- **Origin**: Carved from the fang of the Shadow Wyrm in the Abyssal Depths
- **Quest Required**: None
- **Level Required**: 30
- **Description**: A curved obsidian dagger that seems to absorb light. Its edge shimmers with an oily, dark iridescence.

### Embercleave

- **Rarity**: Legendary

- **Curse**: Wielder becomes vulnerable to ice damage for 30 seconds after use
- **Origin**: Reforged from the ashes of the Phoenix King's last stand
- **Quest Required**: Flames of Retribution
- **Level Required**: 20
- **Description**: A massive two-handed greatsword wreathed in eternal flames. The blade appears to be made of molten metal that never cools.
