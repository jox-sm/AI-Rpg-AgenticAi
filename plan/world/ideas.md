# RPG Ideas & Mechanics Reference

Research compiled from 5 categories: Combat, Items, World, Entities, and Progression.

---

## 1. Combat Mechanics

### Unique Text RPG Combat Systems
- **Shadow Fight 2** - Skill-based combat with combos, blocking, dodging, magic abilities
- **Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup** - Turn-based with hunger system, 20+ weapon types, god abilities
- **Angband** - Permanent death, deep dungeon exploration, artifact collection
- **Caves of Qud** - Mutation system, elemental damage, limb-specific damage
- **Cataclysm DDA** - Real-time with pause, martial arts styles, bionics/mutations
- **Caves Roguelike** - Permadeath, procedurally generated levels, simple text interface
- **Rogue** - The original roguelike with inventory management and floor exploration
- **NetHack** - Complex item interactions, polymorph system, pet companions

### Combat System Ideas
- **Limb-based damage** - Target specific body parts (head, arms, legs) for different effects
- **Stance system** - Switch between offensive/defensive/balanced stances mid-fight
- **Combo chains** - String attacks together for bonus damage
- **Elemental weather combos** - Fire + rain = steam cloud, Lightning + water = AoE
- **Momentum system** - Successive hits build momentum for special abilities
- **Fatigue/stamina** - Attacks and blocks consume energy, rest to recover
- **Weapon arts** - Special moves unique to each weapon type
- **Environmental combat** - Lure enemies into traps, use terrain advantages
- **Surprise round** - First strike advantage based on stealth vs perception
- **Flanking bonuses** - Position-based damage multipliers
- **Grappling** - Close-range combat with throw/trip/choke options
- **Ammo management** - Ranged weapons require resource tracking
- **Cooldown management** - Abilities on timers, strategic timing matters
- **Blood/scent tracking** - Injured enemies leave trails, attract predators

## 2. Item & Crafting Systems

### Unique Item Types
- **Runestones** - Activatable single-use spells
- **Dialectic tools** - Alchemy + crafting hybrid items
- **Monster parts** - Loot from defeated enemies for crafting
- **Scrolls** - One-time use spells or recipes
- **Gems** - Socket into equipment for bonuses
- **Amulets** - Passive buffs, some activatable
- **Soul gems** - Capture enemy essence for enchanting
- **Essence vials** - Elemental ingredients for crafting
- **Ancient artifacts** - Rare items with unique powers
- **Consumable buffs** - Food, potions, drugs with temporary effects

### Crafting Mechanics
- **Recipe discovery** - Find or experiment to learn recipes or pay to skilled blacksmithes
- **Material quality** - Iron < Steel < Mithril < Adamantine
- **Enchanting table** - Combine runes with weapons for effects
- **Salvaging** - Break down items for raw materials
- **Transmutation** - Convert between material types at cost
- **Blessed/cursed items** - Random quality on crafted goods
- **Set bonuses** - Matching armor pieces give extra effects
- **Socket system** - Add gems/runes to equipment slots
- **Durability system** - Items degrade with use, can be repaired
- **Legendary materials** - Rare drops from bosses for ultimate gear

### Unique Affixes (Prefixes/Suffixes)
- **Vampiric** - Steal HP on hit
- **Echoing** - Chance to repeat attack
- **Phasing** - Attacks ignore armor
- **Unstable** - Random bonus effect each hit
- **Symbiotic** - Grows stronger with damage taken
- **Parasitic** - Drains stats from nearby allies
- **Cursed** - Powerful effect but with drawback
- **Berserker** - More damage at lower HP
- **Sentient** - Item has personality, may refuse commands
- **Adaptive** - Changes element to target weakness

- **Living world** - NPCs have schedules, factions war over territory

- **Dynamic weather** - Affects visibility, movement, combat

- **Point of interest system** - Landmarks attract exploration
- **Hidden passages** - Perception-based secret discovery
- **Traps** - Floor spikes, poison darts, collapsing ceilings

- **Climbing/swimming** - Vertical and water exploration
- **Sailing** - Ocean exploration, island discovery
- **Cartography** - Create and trade maps
- **Scouting** - Send scouts ahead to reveal dangers

### Biome Ideas

- **Fungal Forest** - Spore mechanics, mushroom creatures, bioluminescence

## 4. Entity & NPC Systems

### Unique Entity Types
- **Elementals** - Fire/water/earth/air with environmental effects
- **Undead** - Weak to holy, resistant to poison
- **Constructs** - Weak to lightning, immune to mind effects
- **Aberrations** - Reality-warping, sanity effects
- **Fey** - Tricksters, weak to iron
- **Dragons** - Legendary, hoard treasure, element breath
- **Giants** - Slow but devastating, environmental destruction
- **Swarms** - Many weak enemies, immune to slashing
- **Shapeshifters** - Change form, copy abilities
- **Golems** - Animated constructs, weak to specific elements

### NPC Systems
- **Dynamic dialogue** - Context-aware responses
- **Relationship system** - Friendship/rivalry with NPCs
- **Faction reputation** - Affects shop prices, quest availability
- **NPC schedules** - Day/night routines
- **Memory** - NPCs remember player actions
- **Personality traits** - Aggressive, cowardly, greedy, kind
- **Skill progression** - NPCs level up over time
- **Mercenary system** - Hire NPCs for party
- **Companion loyalty** - Party members have approval meters
- **Marriage/family** - Social systems for immersion

### AI Behavior Ideas
- **Need-based AI** - Hunger, safety, social needs drive behavior
- **Pack tactics** - Wolves coordinate, goblins flank
- **Territorial defense** - Enemies protect their lair
- **Fleeing mechanics** - Enemies run when outmatched
- **Bait tactics** - Lure players into ambushes
- **Resource gathering** - NPCs collect food, materials
- **Crafting** - Enemies use items they find
- **Communication** - Alert nearby enemies, call for help
- **Adaptation** - Learn from player tactics
- **Leadership** - Boss enemies buff minions
